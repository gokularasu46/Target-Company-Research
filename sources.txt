# Data Sources and References

1. **Company Official Websites**:
   Primary source of truth for verifying manufacturing locations, product segments, and engineering career opportunities. Used to confirm R&D center locations vs. pure sales offices.

2. **LinkedIn (Company Pages & Employee Profiles)**:
   Used to verify if companies have actual engineering operations in Chennai by checking the presence of Mechanical, ECE, and Instrumentation engineering roles based in the city.

3. **IndiaMART & Trade Directories**:
   Used for initial lead generation of industrial instrumentation and medical equipment manufacturers in Tamil Nadu.

4. **Industry News Portals**:
   - The Hindu Business Line
   - Autocar Professional (for auto-sensors)
   - Electronics For You (EFY)
   - Medical Device Network
   Consulted to verify new manufacturing plant inaugurations (e.g., Pegatron, Salcomp, Foxconn in Sriperumbudur/Chengalpattu).

5. **Government & Industrial Park Listings**:
   - SIPCOT (State Industries Promotion Corporation of Tamil Nadu) maps for Oragadam and Sriperumbudur to verify tenant companies.
   - MEPZ (Madras Export Processing Zone) listings.
